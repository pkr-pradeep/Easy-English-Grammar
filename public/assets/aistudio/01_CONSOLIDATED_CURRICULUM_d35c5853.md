# Consolidated English Grammar Curriculum

## Cross-textbook structure for English Grammar Adventure

This document proposes a unified learning sequence from four reference
textbooks. It is an organisational consolidation for the app, not an
official combined syllabus issued by the boards.

## 1. Reference books and their contents

### A. Assam --- *A New Method English Grammar*, Classes IX--X

The contents pages show the following chapter structure:

1.  Sentence
2.  Phrases and Clauses
3.  Transformation of Sentences
4.  Nouns
5.  Pronouns
6.  Determinatives
7.  Clauses
8.  Analysis
9.  Tense
10. Special Verbs
11. Voice
12. Conjunctives and Relatives
13. Adverbials
14. Position of Prepositions
15. Reported Speech
16. Transformation of Sentences (continued)
17. Punctuation
18. Synonyms
19. Antonyms
20. Similarly pronounced words distinguished in meanings / confusing
    word pairs
21. Simple phrases and idioms
22. Substitution of single words
23. Diminutives

The book includes sentence structure, adjuncts, kinds of sentences,
predicate and complements, complex/compound sentences, phrase and clause
analysis, affirmative/negative transformation, nouns, pronouns,
determinatives, adjectives, noun/adjective/adverbial clauses, tense,
special verbs, voice, conjunctions, relatives, adverbials, prepositions,
reported speech, punctuation and vocabulary.

### B. Punjab --- *A Practice Book of English Grammar & Composition*, Class IX

Grammar contents:

1.  Determiners
2.  Modals
3.  Prepositions
4.  Conjunctions
5.  Simple and Complex Sentences
6.  Voice
7.  Narration
8.  Non-finites
9.  Tenses (Concord and Sequence)

The same contents page also includes composition and language skills:

-   Vocabulary: nature of words/context meaning, words as different

-   Reading skills: prose passages and poems.
-   Writing skills: note-making, short messages, emails, informal and
    formal letters.
-   Paragraph writing: describing people, places, events/incidents and
    procedures.

## 2. Consolidated learning worlds and modules

### World 1 --- The Sentence Kingdom

**Module 1: Sentence Basics and Word Order** - What is a sentence? -
Meaningful word groups. - Sentence structure and word order. - Sentence
elements. - Basic sentence construction.

**Module 2: Subject, Predicate, Adjuncts and Complements** - Subject and
predicate. - Predicate structure. - Complements. - Adjuncts. - Forms of
complement. - Identifying sentence parts.

**Module 3: Types of Sentences and Sentence Structure** - Kinds of
sentences. - Simple, compound and complex sentences. - Main and
subordinate clauses. - Sentence analysis. - Sentence structure patterns.

**Module 4: Phrases and Clauses** - Phrase and clause distinction. -
Phrase types and functions. - Main and subordinate clauses. - Noun
clauses. - Adjective/relative clauses. - Adverbial clauses. - Clause
analysis.

### World 2 --- The Word Zoo

**Module 5: Parts of Speech and Nouns** - Parts of speech. - Nouns and
their kinds. - Number. - Gender. - Case. - Noun usage.

**Module 6: Pronouns** - Pronoun types and usage. - Pronoun reference. -
Correct pronoun choice.

**Module 7: Determiners and Articles** - Articles: a, an, the. -
Demonstratives. - Possessives. - Numerals. - Quantifiers. -
Wh-determiners. - Other determinative forms. - Determiner position and
usage.

**Module 8: Countable and Uncountable Nouns** - Countable nouns. -
Uncountable nouns. - Singular and plural usage. - Quantifiers with
countable/uncountable nouns. - Common errors.

**Module 9: Adjectives** - Adjective functions. - Kinds of adjectives. -
Position and order. - Comparison where covered by the source material. -
Adjectives in noun phrases.

### World 3 --- The Time Machine

**Module 10: Verbs and Verb Groups** - Main verbs. - Verb forms. - Verb
groups. - Regular and irregular forms. - Use of verbs in sentences.

**Module 11: Finite and Non-finite Verbs** - Finite verbs. - Non-finite
verbs. - Infinitives. - Participles. - Gerunds where covered in the
source material. - Verb forms in phrases and clauses.

**Module 12: Auxiliaries, Modals and Special Verbs** - Primary
auxiliaries. - Modal auxiliaries. - Ability, permission, possibility,
obligation and advice. - Special verbs. - Non-conclusive verbs. -
Inchoative verbs. - Group verbs. - Appropriate verb patterns.

**Module 13: Tense, Time and Aspect** - Difference between time and
tense. - Present and past tense forms. - Ways of expressing future
time. - Simple, progressive, perfect and perfect progressive aspects. -
Verb forms and tense structures. - Uses of different tense forms. -
Sequence/concord of tenses where covered.

**Module 14: Subject--Verb Agreement** - Agreement in person and
number. - Singular and plural subjects. - Compound subjects. -
Indefinite expressions. - Collective and special noun cases. - Agreement
in clauses. - Common errors.

### World 4 --- The Word Detective Agency

**Module 15: Noun Phrases and Pre/Post Modifiers** - Noun phrase
structure. - Head noun. - Pre-modifiers. - Post-modifiers. -
Determiners, adjectives, participles and nouns as modifiers. - Expanding
and identifying noun phrases.

**Module 16: Predicate Phrases** - Predicate phrase structure. - Verb
and other predicate elements. - Complements and adjuncts. - Identifying
predicate patterns.

**Module 17: Adverbs and Adverbials** - Adverbs and adverbials. - Types
and functions. - Position of adverbials. - Adverbial phrases and
clauses. - Meaning and emphasis.

**Module 18: Prepositions and Prepositional Objects** - Prepositions and
their objects. - Position of prepositions. - Prepositional phrases. -
Appropriate preposition choice. - Prepositions used with adjectives and
nouns. - Common usage patterns.

**Module 19: Conjunctions and Connecting Words** - Coordinating
conjunctions. - Subordinating conjunctions. - Conjunctives and
relatives. - Correlative forms. - Connecting clauses and ideas.

### World 5 --- The Sentence Courtroom

**Module 20: Negatives and Interrogatives** - Negative sentence
formation. - Interrogative sentence formation. - Question words and
auxiliaries. - Negative forms. - Double negatives where addressed in
source material. - Correct question structures.

**Module 21: Active and Passive Voice** - Active voice. - Passive
voice. - Form and transformation. - Tense changes in passive
constructions. - Passive in different sentence patterns. - Passive with
and without an agent. - Appropriate use of passive.

**Module 22: Direct, Indirect and Reported Speech** - Direct speech. -
Indirect/reported speech. - Reporting verbs. - Statements, questions,
commands and requests. - Exclamations and wishes. - Changes in tense,
pronouns and time/place expressions. - Reporting context and time.

**Module 23: Sentence Transformation and Conditional Structures** -
Affirmative and negative transformation. - Interrogative and other
sentence transformations where covered. - Active/passive
transformation. - Changing parts of speech without changing meaning. -
Simple, compound and complex transformations. - Conditional sentences. -
Concession and contrast. - Miscellaneous transformations.

### World 6 --- The Vocabulary Carnival

**Module 24: Punctuation and Word Formation** - Punctuation. -
Capitalisation and sentence punctuation. - Word formation. -
Substitution of single words. - Diminutives.

**Module 25: Vocabulary, Idioms, Phrasal Verbs and Confusing Words** -
Synonyms. - Antonyms. - Homonyms. - Words with similar pronunciation but
different meanings. - Contextual meaning. - Simple phrases and idioms. -
Phrasal verbs. - Vocabulary in context.

-   Short messages.
-   Emails.
-   Informal letters.
-   Formal letters.
-   Reading comprehension: prose passages and poems.

These topics are explicitly visible in the Punjab Class IX contents. If
other reference books contain related material, map those separately
after checking their chapter text.

## 5. Prerequisite and progression rules

The app should support explicit prerequisite links. Suggested
dependencies:

-   Sentence basics → Subject and predicate → Sentence types → Phrases
    and clauses.
-   Nouns → Number → Subject--verb agreement.
-   Nouns and pronouns → Noun phrases.
-   Determiners → Noun phrase modifiers.
-   Verbs and verb forms → Auxiliaries and modals → Tense and aspect.
-   Verb forms and tense → Subject--verb agreement.
-   Clauses → Noun, relative and adverb clauses.
-   Tense and auxiliaries → Passive voice and reported speech.
-   Sentence structure → Sentence transformation.

These are suggested learning dependencies, not official board
sequencing. The curriculum editor should allow educators to change them.

## 6. Curriculum data rules

Every module and subtopic should carry:

-   Stable topic ID.
-   Consolidated module name.
-   Subtopic name.
-   Source board.
-   Source class.
-   Source textbook title.
-   Original chapter title and number.
-   Source page or page range, if verified.
-   Topic status: shared, source-specific or extension.
-   Prerequisite topic IDs.
-   Learning objectives.
-   Lesson status: planned, drafted, reviewed or published.

Do not fabricate source page references. Mark missing references as "not
verified" until checked against the PDF.
